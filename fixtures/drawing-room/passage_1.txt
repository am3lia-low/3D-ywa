Clara entered the drawing room and set down her case. A tall grandfather clock stood against the north wall, its pendulum still. Beside it, a carved console table held a porcelain tea set on a lacquered tray. An ornate mirror in a gilded frame hung above the marble fireplace, and a worn red rug covered the floorboards in front of the hearth. A leather ottoman sat beside a period armchair, both angled toward the cold grate. On the writing desk near the window rested a brass lantern and an aged leather notebook, its cover swollen with damp.
